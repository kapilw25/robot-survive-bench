# Keep it a SURVEY, not a position paper
Problem: prescriptive phrasing turns a survey into a position paper and undercuts its neutrality.
Fix: describe and ASSESS the field; report the thesis (for example "prediction must earn a closed-loop advantage") as a FINDING, never as the paper's stance. Survey voice is secured by a PRISMA methodology plus a 150-300 work landscape, not a small citation list.
