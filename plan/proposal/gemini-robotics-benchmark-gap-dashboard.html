T2b

The novelty gap, stated as a paper

Every ER benchmark is single-turn. Every memory benchmark targets video world models. GR2 sells long-horizon planning, live redirection, multi-robot collaboration — and nothing measures whether its world-state survives any of them.

The intersection — multi-turn, intervention-robust world-state memory for embodied-reasoning models, evaluated purely by inference — is unoccupied, and extends your survey's memory-consistency taxonomy into the embodied frontier.

T3

Execution: World-Action Models and the distributed skill each unlocks

You want WAMs, not general VLMs. The honest constraint: most WAM policies are small (sub-3B, single-GPU, built for real-time control), so they will not force model sharding. That does not cost you the skill, it moves it to data parallelism. Two practice grounds: (A) small WAMs, where FSDP / DDP + data-parallel sharded eval is exactly the hands-on you get; (B) the large video-foundation backbones WAMs fine-tune from, where model sharding (tensor / expert / full-shard) becomes unavoidable.

T3b

Skill ladder — data parallelism first, then model sharding

Reordered for your reality: the first two rungs are reachable today with the small WAMs above via FSDP/DDP. The upper rungs need the Tier-2 backbones. Each rung is a résumé line and a paper figure.

RUNG 1 · reachable now

Data parallelism (DDP)

Replicate a small WAM across N GPUs and shard the eval workload (episodes) data-parallel. Push aggregate throughput, measure per-GPU utilization. Pure win with sub-3B models.

RUNG 2 · reachable now

FSDP full-shard (ZeRO-3)

Shard params, grads and optimizer state to fine-tune or serve a mid-size WAM past one GPU's memory. Add activation checkpointing and CPU offload. --sharding-strategy FULL_SHARD.

RUNG 3 · needs Tier 2

Tensor / expert parallelism

Split a large video-FM backbone (Cosmos, Summer-22B) across GPUs with all-reduce; expert-parallel for any MoE backbone. True model sharding.

RUNG 4

Quantization, lossless-ish

FP8 / NVFP4 / AWQ / GPTQ to fit big backbones on fewer GPUs. Measure the accuracy delta on your harness — that delta is a paper figure.

RUNG 5

KV-cache, throughput & MFU

Paged attention, continuous batching, prefix caching; then measure model-FLOPs-utilization and tune the comm bottleneck. The language infra interviews speak.

The compounding move · data-parallel eval of these WAMs to run your memory-consistency probes IS the benchmark's infrastructure, and stepping up to shard a Tier-2 backbone adds the model-parallel skill on top. One body of work yields three things: the P0/P1 benchmark paper, an FSDP + distributed-eval résumé line, and a reusable multi-GPU harness. One node, one bill.
